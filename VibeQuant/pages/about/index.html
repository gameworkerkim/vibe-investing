Dennis Kim (김호광)

This is the story of an assembly-generation programmer living in—and observing—the last era when networks still advanced humanity, before they finished parallelizing the human.

Independent researcher at the intersection of cyber threat intelligence, AI-driven quantitative investing, and Web3. Former CEO of Cyworld (Korea's pioneering social platform, 35M users) · Investor · Microsoft Azure MVP (2015–2023, 9 consecutive years).

Over 28 years across game security, social platforms, and blockchain, I turn breaking security and market events into open-source research and software—often within 24–48 hours. Beyond the terminal, I write on art, literature, and culture—exploring the questions technology alone cannot answer.
